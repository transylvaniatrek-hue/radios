Click the upper-right edge of the volume knob to power on / turn up. Click the lower-left edge to turn down; reducing to 0 powers off. The lock switch is only visible from the Top View — locking still lets the knobs and A/B/C switch work, but every button just beeps.

Push-to-Talk

Idle — hold the PTT button to transmit.

Press and hold the PTT bar on the side of the radio. Wait for the confirmation beep, pause briefly, then speak. Pause about half a second before you release — the radio (and this simulator) clips the last bit of every transmission.
